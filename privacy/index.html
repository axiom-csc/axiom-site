Privacy

What we collect

When you sign up for research result notifications, we collect your email address. We do not collect any other personal information.

How we use it

We use your email address only to send you notifications when Axiom Research publishes new research results. We will never use it for marketing, advertising, or any other purpose.

Who sees it

Your email is stored by Formspree, which handles form submissions on our behalf. We do not share your email with any other third party.

Removing yourself

To remove yourself from the notification list at any time, email narensara@usepanel.dev with the subject “Unsubscribe” and your address will be removed.
