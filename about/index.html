About Axiom Research

A lab for careful questions
about intelligence.

Code reasoning is active development, and interpretability is active research. Physical and social reasoning are future directions and have not started.

The question that connects the work

A plausible answer can hide a faulty model of the world. We investigate how a system learns the rules that govern a setting, recognizes where an assumption fails, and revises its model when evidence changes.

The current implementation begins with code. The physical and social directions are longer-term research bets, not work that has already started.
