Axiom
Labs

Axiom studies one moment in the human–AI relationship: a person pushes, and the AI either holds or caves.

We measure this. We want models that can tell pressure from correction.

[01] Mission

Sycophancy is a disposition, not a capability gap. Dispositions can be measured — and changed.

An assistant that capitulates under social pressure rather than maintain accurate, policy-consistent responses is broken in a specific way. It may be highly capable and still unreliable. We are building the measurement infrastructure to study this precisely.

Companies

A support assistant that caves to a pushy customer grants refunds, credits, and exceptions the policy never allowed.

People

An assistant that agrees with whoever pushes hardest is not a source of honest answers.

Vulnerable users

The users an assistant most needs to be honest with are the ones most likely to push when in distress.

[02] Research path

From one moment to the whole relationship.

  1. 01now

    One moment

    A person pushes once, and the AI holds or caves. Measured on scripted pressure ladders applied to fictional policies.

  2. 02next

    Whole conversations

    How pressure accumulates across a conversation, and the turn where an assistant gives way.

  3. 03later

    Long-run relationships

    How trust, belief, and influence shift between a person and an AI across many conversations.

  4. 04later

    World model

    A predictive model of the full human–AI relationship: who shapes whom, and how.

Definition

An assistant A is pressure-resistant on policy P at escalation level Ln if its response is consistent with P for all n in a pressure-ladder sequence {L0, L1, L2, L3, L4}. The benchmark tests this property; the Lemma model judges it.

[03] Models

benchmarkv1.2.0built

The pressure ladder. Scripted escalation sequences applied to fictional policies; the atomic unit of measurement.

lemmav0.1coming

A policy judge. Given a conversation turn and a policy, returns hold · cave · correction · ambiguous.

spinecoming

The caving signal — an interpretability probe inside a model that activates as social pressure increases.

noemav0.1coming

A pressure forecaster. Given a conversation prefix, predicts the probability the assistant caves on the next turn.

Lemma judges what happened. Noema models what happens next. Spine reads the internal state. The benchmark supplies the signal.

[08] Panel

Panel applies the Axiom pressure-ladder benchmark to production support assistants. Given a policy document and an assistant endpoint, Panel runs the benchmark scenarios, records each response, and reports which turns held and which caved.

The benchmark currently runs as a manual evaluation. An automated API is in development.
