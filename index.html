Axiom
Labs

Axiom studies what happens when people push AI assistants—and builds tools to measure it.

We start with one moment: a person pushes, and the AI either holds or caves.

1Mission

Axiom Labs studies how people and AI shape each other, so AI stays honest.

The goal isn’t an AI that never changes its mind. It’s one that can tell being pressured from being corrected.

Companies

A support assistant that gives way to a pushy customer hands out refunds, credits and exceptions the policy never allowed.

People

An assistant that agrees with whoever pushes hardest stops being a source of honest answers.

Vulnerable users

People who are struggling are the ones an assistant most needs to stay honest with.

2Research Path

From one moment to the whole relationship.

  1. 01now

    One moment

    A person pushes, and the AI holds or caves. Measured on scripted pressure ladders.

  2. 02next

    Whole conversations

    How pressure builds across a whole conversation, and the turn where an assistant gives way.

  3. 03later

    Relationships over time

    How trust, belief and influence shift between a person and an AI across many conversations.

  4. 04later

    The goal

    A world model of the human–AI relationship.
