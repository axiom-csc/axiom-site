Useful intelligence is not only a question of scale. It is a question of fit: to the task, the available evidence, and the resources required to deliver it.

We’re building Axiom Labs to investigate efficient, adaptable models—and tools that make their behavior measurable.

1Mission

Make AI practical to specialize, evaluate, and deploy.

Our ambition is to give organizations more control over the intelligence they use. We’re starting with a focused question: how can compact models adapt to changing requirements while preserving the behaviors that matter?

Benchmark Built · Nov 2026

Axiom Benchmarks

Pressure-ladder evaluation across five customer-service domains. 75 published scenarios; sealed test split held for final comparisons. Methodology →

Lemma Roadmap

Deterministic judge

Rule-based verdict engine for benchmark scoring. Evaluates assistant turns against policy constraints with explicit, inspectable logic.

Spine Roadmap

Compact fine-tuned model

Investigating how a compact model acquires policy-holding behavior without requiring a general-purpose scale.

Noema Roadmap

Predictive world model

Models what the user will do next given conversation history and policy state. Enables anticipatory evaluation, not just reactive scoring.
