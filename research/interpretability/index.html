The research focus

Interpretability is active research: analyzing the internal structural shifts behind a modified prediction.

The focus is the structural change associated with the modified prediction, rather than treating the new output as a complete explanation on its own.

What needs to be understood

The research asks what changed internally, how that change relates to the modified prediction, and how to distinguish the observed shift from a stronger causal claim.

Current scope

This is an active research direction, not an announced performance result. No general claim about a validated intervention or a solved interpretability mechanism is being made.
